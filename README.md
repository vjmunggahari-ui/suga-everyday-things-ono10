A two-line explanation of a toaster for a 10-year-old, built on one concrete analogy and an honest account of what that analogy leaves out.

> A toaster is a tiny elevator for bread: it drops your slice down into a hot room, then pops it back up wearing a tan.
> The elevator part fits — down, heat, back up — but an elevator moves people, and a toaster's whole job is the heat, not the ride.

`main.ts` (Node, no dependencies) writes exactly this entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (247 characters, 2 lines) and self-checks the 1–400 character gate, the two-line shape, one sentence per line, no trailing newline, and the entry anchors before writing. It throws if `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` is unset.
